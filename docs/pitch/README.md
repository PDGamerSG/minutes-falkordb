# Pitch materials

This folder holds Team Vulcan's Graph Hacks 2026 pitch for Minutes. Every number in it comes from `README.md`, `docs/overnight-report.md`, `docs/bench/README.md` and `data/meetings/README.md`. `pitch-script.md` is the 3:00 narrated video script: timestamped rows, a recording checklist and eight speaking notes. It is ready to rehearse; the narration runs 378 words at no more than 2.3 words per second, and the live URL is still a placeholder. `minutes-pitch.pptx` is the 12-slide deck. It opens in PowerPoint and passes package validation, and all 12 slides were exported to PDF through PowerPoint and checked for overflow. Slide 12 carries the repo link and points to the README for the live demo and video. `video-storyboard.md` is the shot list for the screen recording, followed by the capture, narration, edit, export and upload steps. It is ready, but nothing has been recorded yet. Two things must happen before recording: fill in `<LIVE_URL>` once a hosted deployment is verified, and confirm the public repository URL.
