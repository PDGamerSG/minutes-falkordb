# Minutes — connected memory

The user rejected the initial small-type, pale-box dashboard. Replace that visual world. This iteration follows the explicit Chrome/Monolith references, led by the user’s confirmed Chrome choice. The skill's randomized directions do not override those supplied references.

## Art direction

An uninterrupted graphite stage holds an original polished, continuous metal knot: many strands sharing one path. Silver catches broad studio highlights; one copper strand carries the selected layer. No confetti, glow cloud, star field, or pale 3D card. The sculpture is illustrative and labeled in its accessible description.

## System

- Ink #101113, raised surface #1a1b1e, fine boundary #303236, foreground #f1f0ec, quiet text #a5a6ab, copper #ed946d.
- Self-hosted variable Inter for headings, body and controls; medium-weight headings, normal body tracking, and 14–16px functional text. The user rejected the wider Syne treatment. Tight but legible display tracking, normal body tracking.
- Home: asymmetrical, generous two-column composition; metal form leads the right side, clear workspace entry on the left. Workspace selection is a compact horizontal directory.
- Workspace: quiet 224px navigation, full-width introduction with connected-memory sculpture, readable operational list below. Content types have different hierarchy; no equal promotional boxes.
- Chat: a focused reading column, strong composer, sources and queries revealed on demand.
- Primary actions use neutral off-white surfaces, 7–8px corners and 36/40/44px control sizes; copper signals selection/status. The oversized pill treatment was rejected.
- The sculpture rotates continuously at 0.22 radians/second (one turn in about 29 seconds), with a small secondary tilt. Pause stops the animation; dragging changes its orientation. Reduced motion starts paused, with an explicit play override.
- Selection and status have text labels. Keyboard controls mirror canvas actions. Dragging adjusts the sculpture; motion pauses offscreen, on hidden tabs, and under reduced-motion preferences. Mobile changes composition instead of shrinking labels.
